I shut down my homelab for months.
Not because it was too hard. Because I was doing it wrong.

First attempt: Proxmox + Talos Linux on a VM provider.
Every node failure meant manual rebuilds. One by one. Again and again.

I even tried wrapping everything in custom Docker containers to manage my tools.
Good idea. Terrible execution. It became a mess fast.

So I stopped. Work, life, discouragement — the usual.

Then I restarted with one rule:
One hour a day. Even if progress feels tiny.

That rhythm changed everything.
No pressure. Just consistent movement forward.

The lesson I wish I'd learned earlier:
A homelab isn't a sprint. It's a long game.
The setup that makes it sustainable matters more than the setup that looks impressive.

Now I'm on attempt #2 — with devcontainers, IaC, and a workflow that actually makes me want to keep going.

Have you ever restarted a project you'd abandoned? What made you go back?
