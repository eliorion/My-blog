I was stuck every time I created a new blog post.
Then one organizational system fixed it.

The system: Johnny Decimal.

I was obsessing over categories and tags. Every new post triggered decision paralysis: does this belong under DevOps? Homelab? Both? Neither?

Johnny Decimal showed me I was thinking about it wrong.

I switched from category-first to date-first:

2025-09-01 - First-step-in-homelab
2025-12-11 - New-homelab-workflow
2026-03-27 - Refactoring-my-blog

No conflicts. No ambiguity. Natural sort order built in.

The date answers 'when'. The title answers 'what'. That's all I need.

Bonus: I already use the same logic in my personal notes. Same mental model everywhere = less friction.

Sometimes the right fix isn't a better tool. It's a better thinking framework.
