I watched every Kubernetes tutorial on YouTube.
I still didn't understand it.

Not really.

I could recite the theory: pods, deployments, services, ingress.
But I couldn't tell you WHY it matters. Or what breaks when it breaks.

Then I built a homelab. Everything changed.

The moment I misconfigured a Talos Linux node and had to debug why the cluster wouldn't form — I learned more in 2 hours than in 10 hours of tutorials.

Breaking things is how you learn infrastructure.

Not watching. Not reading. Breaking.

If you're stuck in tutorial hell on Kubernetes — stop.
Get a cheap mini PC. Install Proxmox. Spin up some VMs. Build the cluster.

You'll break it.
That's the point.
