I'm a software engineer. I build systems every day.
But I had no idea how Netflix serves 50 million people at the same time without crashing.

That question haunted me.

So I stopped reading about it. I started building.

One mini PC. A Nipogi E3B — 16GB RAM, under 300€.
Proxmox on top for VMs. Talos Linux for the Kubernetes nodes.

Suddenly the theory clicked.

Resource allocation, node scheduling, pod restarts — not abstract concepts anymore.
Real systems I could break, debug, and fix myself.

If you're a software engineer who's never run your own cluster: this is the gap.

You don't need a cloud budget.
You need a cheap box and the willingness to get your hands dirty.

The homelab is just the beginning.
