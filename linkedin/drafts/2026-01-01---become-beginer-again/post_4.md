Schools teach you what to learn.
Nobody teaches you how to learn.

I spent months spinning my wheels — learning things, forgetting them, starting over, losing direction.

Then I stumbled onto something obvious in retrospect:

Without a system for tracking progress and setting goals, effort is just noise.

I now use:
→ SMART goals at every time scale (daily → weekly → monthly → yearly)
→ Obsidian for structured notes with vim bindings
→ Weekly reviews to catch drift before it compounds
→ A yearly vision note — not a todo list, but a desired state

The yearly note is the most important.

It doesn't track what I did. It defines who I want to become.

Everything else exists to answer: "Does this move me toward that?"

Two months with this system and the difference is night and day.

Not because I'm working harder.

Because I stopped working on the wrong things.
