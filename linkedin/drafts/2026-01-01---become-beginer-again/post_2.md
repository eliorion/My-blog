I deleted my mouse from my workflow.
Here's what replaced it.

After joining a Linux career program, I redesigned my entire dev setup around one constraint:

If I can't do it with a keyboard, I won't do it.

Here's the stack:

→ tmux — terminal multiplexer, replaces every "open a new tab" reflex
→ vim — text editor that lives in the terminal
→ Touch typing — proper 10-finger technique, no looking at keys
→ macOS keyboard shortcuts — navigate desktops without touching the trackpad

Am I slower right now? Absolutely.

I type like I'm learning a new language — because I am.

But here's the thing about muscle memory: once it clicks, it never goes away.

The engineers I admire most all share one trait: they don't fight their tools.

They've internalized them.

That's what I'm building toward.
