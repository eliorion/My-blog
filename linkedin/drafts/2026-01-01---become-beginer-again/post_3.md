I learned more in 2 months than in the previous 10.
One system made the difference.

It's not a productivity app. It's not a course.

It's a structured note-taking system in Obsidian:

Daily notes — what did I do today?
Weekly notes — am I on track? What tasks slipped?
Monthly notes — where do I stand on my goals? How do I feel?
Yearly note — what state do I want to reach this year?

Each layer feeds into one question:

"How do I get to where I want to be?"

For goals, I use SMART:
→ Specific — what exactly will I do?
→ Measurable — how will I know I succeeded?
→ Achievable — do I have the skills and resources?
→ Relevant — does this align with my bigger vision?
→ Time-bound — by when?

The result?

Even on low-motivation days, I don't stop. I know exactly why I'm doing this and what the next step is.

I genuinely don't understand why this isn't taught in school.
