Waiting until your setup is 'good enough' to share?
That's how projects die quietly.

I did this for years.

I had a mental checklist: get a dedicated server, configure a real domain, build something polished enough to show.

None of it happened. Life intervened. Priorities shifted.
The projects stayed on my machine.

The irony? The technical barrier was never real.
Hugo + GitHub Pages lets you ship a blog in an afternoon — for free, with a proper domain and CI/CD.

The real barrier was deciding that what I was building was worth sharing.

I've crossed that line now.

Starting this blog not because everything is perfect, but because shipping beats hoarding.
